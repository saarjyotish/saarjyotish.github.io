About the App

SaarJyotish (सारज्योतिष) is a Vedic astrology app for Android, available in Hindi and English. It is published by Saar Jyotish, a proprietorship firm registered with the Ministry of MSME, Government of India (Udyam Registration No. UDYAM-MH-01-0324528), Maharashtra, India.

  • Birth chart (kundali) with Lagna, Chandra, Chalit and divisional charts, dasha and transits
  • Guna Milan and multi-level kundali matching
  • Answers to life questions: marriage, career, education, health, property and more
  • Muhurta (auspicious time) for griha pravesh, land, vehicle and business, with choghadiya
  • Daily, weekly and monthly rashifal, fasting calendar and birth time rectification

Astrology describes possibilities; it is not a substitute for medical, legal, financial or other professional advice. Users must be 18 years or older to make a payment in the App.

3. What We Collect

3.1 Account identity

If you create an account: your mobile number and/or email address, its verification status, account creation timestamp, and (only if you separately, explicitly opt in via its own un-pre-ticked checkbox) marketing-consent status/version/timestamp. We never send a real OTP code to your device from our servers in production without your request for it — the OTP itself is never logged in a human-readable way beyond a short-lived hashed comparison, and expires automatically.

If you instead sign in with Google Sign-In (an alternative, free-to-you login path), we receive only your Google account's email address and Google's own confirmation that it is verified; we do not receive your Google password or any other Google account data, and manually typing an email elsewhere in the App is never treated as equivalent to this Google-confirmed verification. See Section 7.5 for this path's current activation status.

3.2 Birth-chart / Kundali data

Stored on your own device. When you request a result, it is sent to our server for that calculation, and the calculation itself does not save it. It is kept on our server only in these cases:

  • Saved-kundali backup: if you log in with an email/Google account, your saved kundali list is backed up to your account so you can restore it on another device. Deleting the account deletes it.
  • Purchase history: a paid question or report keeps the question title and the chart(s) it was bought for (name, birth date, time and place), so you can reopen the answer within its valid period, also on another device. Deleting the account clears these details; the payment row itself (fee, amount, date) stays for accounting (Section 9).
  • BTR research data: see the Terms section "BTR research data".

3.3 Your questions and AI Guruji conversation content

When you use the paid A.I. गुरुजी chat, your messages and the text of the answer you are asking about are sent to our AI provider (Section 7.4) to write the reply; the reply passes our own filters before you see it. The answer text is sent as it appears in the App, so it can include a name or chart details shown in that answer; no other account data (email, mobile, wallet) is sent. Your messages and the replies are saved on our server with that paid unlock, so the conversation can continue and the message limit can be enforced. If you are logged in, deleting your account deletes these chats; otherwise you can ask us (Section 2) to delete them.

3.4 Transaction/payment and wallet information

Fee type, amount, status, the question it relates to, and (if you were logged in at purchase time) a link to your account so the purchase is recoverable if you sign in on a new device. Wallet activity is a full, auditable transaction ledger (credits/debits/ refunds), never a bare mutable balance.

3.5 Device/session/security information

For admin/Jyotishi-tier users: a login-session token and 2FA state. For end-user accounts: a revocable session token. For crash reporting: an error message, stack trace, platform, app version, and an opaque per-install session id — never linked to any device identifier or personal data.

3.6 Consent records

Marketing consent (Section 3.1) and Terms/Privacy acceptance are each their own explicit, timestamped, versioned record — never implied by account creation.

3.7 Place search, location, voice and uploads

Place search sends the text you type to Google (Section 7.6) through our server, only to suggest place names. "Current location" is read on your device only when you tap it. Voice typing uses your phone's own speech-recognition service (on Android usually Google's), and spoken replies use your phone's own text-to-speech. Biodata PDFs/photos you upload are read on our server to fill the form and are not saved.

4. Purposes of Processing

Account identity: to authenticate you and let you recover purchases/wallet balance across devices. Birth-chart data: to compute the result you requested and, where Section 3.2 says so, to back up your saved kundalis and reopen what you bought. A.I. गुरुजी chat: to answer your follow-up questions about that answer. Payment/ wallet data: to grant, verify, and let you review your entitlements. Crash reports: to fix bugs.

5. Legal Basis / Consent

Account creation and OTP verification are based on your consent (by using the login feature) and legitimate necessity to provide the service you requested. Marketing communications are based solely on your separate, explicit, revocable consent (Section 6).

5.1 Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — Current Status

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 has been enacted, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 were notified in the Gazette on 14 November 2025. Under the notified commencement schedule, most substantive compliance obligations under the Act and Rules come into force in phases, with full applicability from 14 May 2027. We do not claim that the App is "fully DPDP-compliant" as if every provision were already legally in force — that would overstate the current legal position. What we do represent is that we have already built, and describe in this Policy, data-protection-aligned practices consistent with the Act's principles (purpose limitation, a genuine consent record, an erasure/account-deletion mechanism, and a grievance contact) ahead of the Act's full commencement, and we will update this Policy as further provisions come into force or as our own practices change.

6. Consent Withdrawal

You may withdraw marketing consent at any time from within the app (the same toggle used to grant it); this takes effect immediately and is recorded with its own timestamp/version.

7. Service Providers / Processors

7.1 Hosting infrastructure: our backend and database are hosted on Railway (Railway Corporation), currently provisioned in Railway's US (San Francisco) region (astrologyapp-server-production.up.railway.app). Railway acts as our data processor for whatever the backend stores (see Section 3) -- it does not independently use your data for its own purposes.

7.2 OTP/SMS/email delivery: not yet integrated — no real vendor receives your mobile number/email for OTP delivery today (per an owner decision, SMS/OTP vendor selection is not required for the current product scope); this section will name the actual vendor and their data-handling terms if that changes.

7.3 Payment processor: payments actually processed today go through a non-production test/mock flow only. A real payment-gateway adapter (Razorpay) exists in our backend (order-creation, signature-verified webhooks) but is not yet activated with live credentials and the App is not yet wired to use it. This section will name Razorpay (or whichever processor actually goes live) and disclose their data-handling terms before real payments begin, not after.

7.4 AI provider: the A.I. गुरुजी chat uses Anthropic, PBC (USA) through its commercial API. It receives only what Section 3.3 describes, to write the reply. Under Anthropic's published commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models.

7.5 Google Sign-In: built into the App as an alternative, no-paid-vendor login path (Google's own free Sign-In service — Google LLC acts as your identity provider, not as our data processor for anything beyond that one-time sign-in confirmation). Active in production.

7.6 Google Places: place search for birth/event places sends the text you type to Google's Places API through our server, only to return matching place names.

8. Security

Server-side OTP hashing (never stored/logged in plain text), rate-limiting on authentication/OTP endpoints, revocable session tokens with expiry, two-factor authentication for admin/Jyotishi accounts, CORS restrictions, and fail-closed production startup checks. We do not claim any specific encryption-at-rest mechanism unless independently verified as actually implemented.

9. Retention and Deletion

  • Birth-chart/Kundali data: on your device until you delete it; the saved-kundali backup and purchase-history details (Section 3.2) until you delete your account.
  • A.I. गुरुजी chats: kept with the paid unlock; deleted with your account, or on request (Section 2).
  • Account identity (mobile/email/verification/consent): retained while your account is active; irreversibly cleared upon account deletion (Section 12) — not merely hidden.
  • Transaction/wallet records: retained for accounting/dispute- resolution/legal-compliance purposes for the period required by applicable law, but no longer linked to your identity once your account is deleted.
  • Crash reports: a reasonable debugging window; never linked to your identity.
